# Flexible SOM fluxes

Description of the flexible SOM fluxes that users may change with
[`memc_configure`](https://microbial-explicit-model.github.io/MEMC/reference/memc_configure.md)
function.

## Details

- DOMuptake:

  dissolved organic uptake by microbial biomass, the flux describing the
  DOM that is broken down for microbial biomoass uptake.

- POMdecomp:

  particulate oranic matter decomposition, the flux describing the POM
  that is broken down into DOM.

- MBdecay:

  microbial biomass decay, the flux descirbing the micorbial biomass
  mortality/turn over.
